01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

YOUR CRICKET / PERSONAL PHOTO
The journey doesn't end at the boundary.

A little more about me.

Cricket has been a huge part of my life for as long as I can remember. Having been around the game since I was two and having played competitive cricket for six years, I've learnt that the game is about much more than what happens on the field. In fact, I believe the actual game is played off it.

Cricket has taught me resilience, patience perseverance and the importance of staying calm under pressure. More than anything, it has taught me that things don't always go according to plan and what matters is how you respond. Ever since my journey on the field ended, my journey off the field began, as I started chasing my dream of becoming a sports analyst. After all, so much of the game is first played on the drawing board before it unfolds on the field.

Beyond cricket, I'm someone who enjoys exploring different places, taking part in adventure activities and making memories along the way. I value the experiences that teach me something, the people I meet and the little moments that often end up meaning the most. Whether it's taking on a new challenge, learning something completely unfamiliar or simply spending time doing what I love, I believe there's always something to take away from every experience.

I truly believe that the little things in life are often taken for granted, when they're actually the things we should cherish the most.
